Eloelektroniikka: kokeellista elektronimusiikkia  
19.8.2026 / 17.00–19.00  
Galleria G12, Annankatu 16, 00120 Helsinki  
  
Tervetuloa kuuntelemaan kokeellista elektronimusiikkia ja tutustumaan Tiitus Petäjäniemen Oletko nähnyt tätä kissaa? -näyttelyyn Galleria G12:een keskiviikkona 19.8.2026. Esitykset alkavat kello 17.00. Tapahtumaan on vapaapääsy.  
  
Welcome to listen to experimental electronic music and to see Tiitus Petäjäniemi's Oletko nähnyt tätä kissaa? exhibition to Gallery G12 on Wednesday 19.8.2026. The performances start at 17.00. Admission to the event is free.  
  
Esiintyjät / performers:  
  
Ilpo Numminen is a synthesizer artist who respects the tradition of craftmanship in electronic music. He also teaches and introduces modular synthesizers to people.  
[https://www.thegoldmine.net/videos/ilpo-numminen/](https://www.thegoldmine.net/videos/ilpo-numminen/)  
  
Marloes van Son creates atmospheric soundscapes with (self-built) synths, vocals, field recordings, occasional crude beats and sometimes clarinet. She is inspired by interactions between humans and technology, which she explores through everyday sounds, objects and interfaces.  
[www.marloesvanson.nl](http://www.marloesvanson.nl)  
  
Koho/Koskenlahti is a duo formed by J. Koho, who works extensively with sound, and percussionist Minna Koskenlahti. The duo is interested in exploring the sonic potential stored in conventional instruments and various sound sources and seeking different ways to unleash it.  
[https://isthmusrec.bandcamp.com/album/kes-n-korvalla](https://isthmusrec.bandcamp.com/album/kes-n-korvalla)
