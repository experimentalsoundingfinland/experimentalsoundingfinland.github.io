DELPHINE DORA (FRA)  
  
Delphine Dora is a French pianist, organist, vocalist, improviser, and composer. For nearly two decades, she has cultivated a distinctive and intuitive musical language that bridges improvisation, folk, contemporary classical, experimental and electroacoustic music, as well as poetic expression. Situated at the confluence of composition and improvisation, her work unfolds through a singular and highly accomplished practice of instant composition, where form emerges in the immediacy of performance. Deeply anchored in the present moment, it explores altered temporalities and sensitive geographies, weaving resonant connections between the contemporary and the archaic, the poetic and the ineffable, the physicality of sound and the ethereal, the outer world and inner life. Her creative process embraces openness, chance, and collaboration. Dora’s music has been released on labels such as Recital, Modern Love, Morc, Okraïna, Feeding Tube, and three:four. She has performed extensively across France, Europe, Japan, and Georgia.  
  
PIIA RINNE  
  
Piia Rinne is a Pori-based sound artist and textile designer who operates at the intersection of sonic experimentation and tactile craftsmanship, weaving a profound connection between these two mediums. Her sound art, largely constructed from tape loops, becomes a metaphor for the textile artist’s craft - cutting, stitching, and layering materials, Rinne shapes sound in much the same way she might approach fabric, imbuing each element with texture and depth.
