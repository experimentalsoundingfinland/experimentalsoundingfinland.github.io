Morph — to undergo a gradual transformation in shape, form, or character, becoming something else.  
  
MORPH Ensemble explores the space between contemporary classical music, experimental composition, and through improvisation.  
Through shifting textures, unconventional structures, and the interaction of acoustic and electric instruments, the ensemble approaches music as a constantly evolving form — one that can transform, dissolve, and reappear in unexpected ways.  
Welcome to the concert! In this performance, MORPH Ensemble presents original compositions by Gon, exploring the relationship between written material, spontaneous interaction, and the possibilities of sound. Music in transformation. Forms in motion.  
  
MORPH Ensemble  
Heliä Viirakivi — Voice & Flute  
Jonathan Bäckström — Contrabass  
Gon Muruaga — Electric Guitar & Compositions
