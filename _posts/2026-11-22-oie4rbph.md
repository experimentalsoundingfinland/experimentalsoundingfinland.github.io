Nuo Nuohoojat consists of Jaakko Kuikka, Melik Turunen and Johanna Vahtola. Their music deals with the dimensions of embodiment and the harmony between humans and machines by exploring how to abstract the immanent naturalness of vocals and shakuhachi, and by exploring the contexts in which the mechanics of a synthesizer can be experienced as non-industrial.
