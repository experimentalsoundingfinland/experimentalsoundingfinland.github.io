Dancensound is a new festival for music and dance improvisation with the first edition happening at late November '26.  
  
Over two evenings, the festival will showcase a curated programme featuring twelve Helsinki-based performers, three dancers and three musicians each evening. The goal of the festival is to create a meeting place for the experimental music and dance communities in Helsinki, bringing together artists across generation, styles, and backgrounds.  
  
PERFORMERS  
Abdissa Assefa, Giorgio Convertito, Linda Holma, Mikko Innanen, Jukka Kääriäinen, Riikka Lakea, Vera Lapitskaya, Libero Mureddu, Liisa Pentti, Livia Schweizer, Terhi Vaimala, Josefiina Vannesluoma  
  
Dancensound is curated by:  
Libero Mureddu & Giorgio Convertito.
