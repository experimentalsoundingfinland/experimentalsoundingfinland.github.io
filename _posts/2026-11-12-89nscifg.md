Latvian guitarist and composer Edgars Rubenis visits Finland in November '26 for 5 shows.   
  
  
The founding member of Riga's experimental drone rock band Mona de Bo, in his solo practice Rubenis now works with traditions of blues and ragtime creating contemporary new music for acoustic guitar.  
  
Since his 2020 acoustic drift, Rubenis’ music has been featured on London's Resonance FM radio, in concerts along with fellow guitarists Gwenifer Raymond, Liam Grant, de Vlaamse Primitieven, Eric Arn, Kadonnut Manner, Andrew DR Abbott, and on various festivals and tours in UK, France, Sweden, Finland, Belgium, The Netherlands and the Baltics.  
  
– review of Shelterbelt by Jakub Knera, The Quietus  
Edgars Rubenis, after years immersed in experimental music, turned to writing for acoustic guitar during the pandemic. What began as a personal exploration of fingerpicked steel string traditions gradually evolved into Pains And Boogies – a trilogy rooted in a contemporary dialogue with early blues and ragtime forms. His compositions pay homage to the originators of the genres, yet repeatedly drift into unfamiliar territory, balancing the influence of the American Primitive school with a highly personal musical language of his own. On Shelterbelt, the persistent tape his, room noise, timing imperfections, and recording imperfections are essential compositional elements. These limitations sharpen its emotional and structural clarity, revealing the skeletal beauty of Rubenis' guitar work. The album's rough sonic character resists the hyperreal precision of contemporary production. It feels like a transmission from somewhere outside linear time: intimate,  spectral, and deeply human.
