never heard, seen, been . . . .

Yuri Landman (NL)  +   
Wilson Tanner Smith (US-FI), Hadi Hrekes (SY) Joni Vierre(FI)  
  
//  
  
 "Instruments you've never seen, sounds you've never heard."  
Yuri Landman (NL) is a experimental luthier, musician and graphic novelist (b. 1973, Zwolle). Renowned for his one-of-a-kind electric string instruments, he has crafted creations for artists such as Sonic Youth's Lee Ranaldo, Liars, Half Japanese's Jad Fair, Liam Finn and Laura-Mary Carter.  
Blurring the lines between music, invention and visual art, Landman has carved out a singular world of experimental sound.  
@Rahina-klubi he appears solo and joined by two local collaborators: Katri of AikaPoliisi and Bejota Sanchez.  
  
//  
  
Wilson Tanner Smith  (cello), is joined for the first time with two masters of strong global and ethnic music traditions, Joni Vierre (guitar) and Hadi Hrekes (percussion).  
  
Hadi Hrekes is a percussionist and improviser of Syrian origin, whose playing reflects Arabic and ethno-Oriental traditions. His primary instruments are the darbuka and the dohola. He is a member of groups including the Sofra Trio.  
  
Joni Vierre is a  Finnish composer, musician, and guitarist/mandolinist. His music draws from sources including jazz, rock, and global musical traditions. He leads the ensemble YHTÄ, among other projects, and serves as an instructor in the Global Music Department at the Sibelius Academy.  
   
Wilson Tanner Smith,  a composer, improviser, cellist and music-theatre artist, is currently based in Helsinki. Besides his focus in experimental music and performance-making, he runs an experimental music series "Something Else" @ Musta Kissa Bar in Helsinki.
