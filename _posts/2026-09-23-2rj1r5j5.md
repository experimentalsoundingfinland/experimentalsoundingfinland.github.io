Libero Mureddu  
  
Libero Mureddu is an Italian and Finnish pianist, improviser, and composer based in Helsinki. With thirty years of experience across the European music scene, his artistic practice explores the intersection of free improvisation, algorithmic processes, and music technology. Currently, he leads the murmur trio with Andrea Grossi and Cristiano Calcagnile, and performs with the quartet Mother’s Delight, with Jukka Kääriäinen, Harri Sjöström, and Heikki Nikula. He also maintains regular duo collaborations with artists such as Charlotte Hug and Corinna Eikmeier. Alongside performing, Mureddu teaches contemporary free and electroacoustic improvisation and is a doctoral researcher at the Sibelius Academy.  
  
Libero Mureddu - piano and electronics

bäckström wärnheim  
bäckström wärnheim is the fruit of a long friendship and musical companionship. Their debut album ett was released in 2025. As intimate as a confidential conversation, their music unfolds with precision and presence while remaining in the spectrum between low and ultralow dynamics. Their carefully constructed musical microverses utilizes the extended sound palettes of their instruments though breath, noise and consonance and invites the listener to an intense listening experience.  
  
Jonathan Bäckström – double bass  
Marcus Wärnheim – alto saxophone
