Moi Darkness! An Evening of improvised music to welcome the dark November.  
  
  
  
Join us to honor the arrival of the dark season through sound and improvisation.  
  
  
  
In the first set Libero Mureddu will open the evening with his stellar solo set.  
  
  
  
  
  
After that Livia & Luciana will dig into an immersive duo journey that starts from closed eyes and dimmed lights.   
  
Closed eyes, dark nights, black.   
  
After all, what is black? Isn’t it the absorption of all the colors?  
  
  
  
Come with us, let’s make the best of these darkening evenings, and let’s see If we find all the colors hidden within.  
  
  
  
  
  
Libero Mureddu, keyboard and electronics  
  
  
  
Luciana G.Álvarez, piano and more  
  
  
  
Livia Schweizer, flute and more

Libero Mureddu is an Italian and Finnish pianist, improviser, composer, and educator based in Helsinki. With thirty years of experience across the European music scene, his artistic practice explores the intersection of free improvisation, algorithmic processes, and music technology. Currently, he leads the murmur trio with Andrea Grossi and Cristiano Calcagnile, and performs with the quartet Mother’s Delight, with Jukka Kääriäinen, Harri Sjöström, and Heikki Nikula. He also maintains regular duo collaborations with artists such as Charlotte Hug and Corinna Eikmeier. Alongside performing, Mureddu teaches contemporary free and electroacoustic improvisation and is a doctoral researcher at the Sibelius Academy.  
  
  
  
  
luciana garcía is a pianist, visionary composer, improviser, and doctoral researcher from Santiago de Chile, based in Helsinki. luciana's work brings together music and commitment to social justice, creating spaces that reimagine new possibilities of existence towards a queer flourishing  
  
  
  
Livia Schweizer is a Helsinki-based flutist, improviser, and artistic researcher dedicated to the intersection of contemporary performance, community engagement, and intercultural dialogue. Originally from Italy, she moved to Helsinki following her graduation from the Conservatory of Livorno to study at the Sibelius Academy with Mikael Helasvuo. Currently a doctoral researcher at Uniarts' Sibelius Academy, Livia is driven by a deep curiosity for non-conventional notation and alternative definitions of the musical score. She is an active improviser and she is one of the co-founder of the Helsinki based new music ensemble Earth Ears Ensemble
