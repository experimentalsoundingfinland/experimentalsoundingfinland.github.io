Line-up:  
Sergio Castrillón - sercello  
Jukka Kääriäinen - guitar  
Harri Sjöström - Saxophone  
  
The IMPROPER Tones club series brings the sounds of free improvisation and experimental music to Odessa, located in connection with Corona Bar! The evenings feature an energetic, unscripted fireworks display of notes, in which elements familiar from contemporary music and free jazz join hands in a dialogue between Finnish and international top musicians.  
  
The musical programming of IMPROPER Tones is curated by guitarist Jukka Kääriäinen, who also performs at our events with a rotating cast of collaborators.
