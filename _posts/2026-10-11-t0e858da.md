Arists:  
Fumantum is the alias of Martin Sepp, based in Tallinn, Estonia. Artist and producer who works in such genres as drone, dark ambient, noise, folk. Also specializes in creating their own ''DIY'' musical instruments.   
Cold and deep soundscapes with a touch of metal clanging makes it dark and restless drone orchestra that keeps listener in suspense. Fumantum gives you the opportunity to experience a dream in which not everyone would like to be.   
  
Antoine Verhaverbeke plays an electric guitar. His live compositions drift slowly through sustain, feedback, overtones, bleeps, rumbles, and whatever accidents happen along the way.  
  
Nick Cavazos is an audio/video artist highly interested in the topic of feedback and its impact on artistic practice through the usage of no-input mixing techniques alongside various DIY video/audio instruments.
