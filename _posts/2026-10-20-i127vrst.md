New acoustic and electronic solo works performed by Jaakko Arola, Jekaterina Viltšenko and Otso Kauniskangas.  
  
Jaakko Arola is a musician, improviser, composer and instrument-maker from Finland. Arola plays multiple traditional and western woodwind instruments. His current practice is centred around expanding the acoustic timbral possibilities of the bass clarinet via a combination of extended techniques and handcrafted mouthpieces.  
[www.jaakkoarola.com](http://www.jaakkoarola.com)  
  
Jekaterina Viltšenko is a Helsinki-based sound designer, composer, and artist. Their work spans a wide range of genres and practices, including electroacoustic composition, spatial audio, live performance, and incidental music composition and sound design for drama and performance.  
[https://vilts.ch/](https://vilts.ch/)  
  
Conceived between late 2025 and early 2026, Dental Boid seeks to compose the monolithic presence of the sound object.  
[www.otsokauniskangas.xyz](http://www.otsokauniskangas.xyz)
