20–21 November, Resonance Festival takes over Caisa for two days of experimental music, live electronics, audiovisual, dance, circus, installation and a few more things.  
  
This year’s edition, Threshold States, looks at moments of transition between places, identities, bodies, systems and ways of being, when familiar structures begin to loosen and what follows is still taking shape.  
  
The evening programme runs in Caisa’s Main Hall from 18:00–23:00 on both days, with a free afternoon programme in the Auditorium/Kino from 12:00–17:00.  
  
After party takes place in bar  
  
The full programme, artist information and tickets are now online via the link in bio:  
[fluxcultassoc.org/resonance/2026-program](http://fluxcultassoc.org/resonance/2026-program)
