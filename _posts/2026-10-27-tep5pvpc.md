Three outstanding Argentinian musicians come together in Helsinki for a unique evening of contemporary jazz and free improvisation.  
  
Trio Integral brings together guitarist Gon Muruaga, saxophonist Luis Nacht and drummer Sergio Beresovsky. Drawing from jazz tradition while constantly pushing beyond it, the trio creates music where intricate harmonic language, adventurous improvisation and dynamic rhythmic interplay meet. Every performance is spontaneous and unique, balancing structure with freedom and lyrical expression with exploration.  
  
The evening opens with a solo performance by Finnish vocalist and flutist Heliä Mailiis Viirakivi, who later joins Trio Integral as a special guest.  
  
Expect an intimate concert where composition and improvisation merge into an unpredictable musical journey.  
  
ARTISTS  
Gon Muruaga – guitar  
Argentinian guitarist, composer and improviser based in Helsinki. His work moves between contemporary music, experimental jazz and free improvisation, with a strong focus on sound, interaction and exploration.  
Luis Nacht – saxophone  
One of Argentina’s leading jazz musicians, internationally recognized as a saxophonist, flutist and composer. His work spans contemporary jazz and free improvisation, with performances throughout Europe and the Americas.  
Sergio Beresovsky – drums  
Argentinian drummer based in Zürich, specializing in improvised music and contemporary jazz. His playing brings a highly interactive and creative approach to rhythm and ensemble communication.  
Special guest: Heliä Mailiis Viirakivi – voice & flute  
Finnish vocalist, flutist, composer and improviser working across contemporary and experimental music, free improvisation and extended vocal techniques.  
  
The concert is part of the Kokeellisen Musiikin ilta concert series.
