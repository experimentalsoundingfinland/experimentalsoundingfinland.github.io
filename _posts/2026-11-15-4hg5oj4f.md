Welcome to an evening of experimental music featuring an international quartet exploring the possibilities of sound through voice, flute, electric guitar, double bass and drums.  
  
Moving between shifting textures, intensity and silence, four distinct musical voices meet without a predetermined direction. Through collective listening and spontaneous interaction, the music takes shape and transforms in the moment.  
  
Line-up  
Heliä Mailiis Viirakivi — voice & flute  
Finnish vocalist, flutist, composer and improviser working with contemporary and experimental music, free improvisation and extended vocal techniques. Picture credit Roman Odjinud.  
Gon Muruaga — electric guitar  
Argentinian guitarist, composer and improviser based in Helsinki. His work moves between contemporary music, experimental jazz and free improvisation.  
Jonathan Bäckström — double bass  
Finnish bassist, composer and improviser based in Helsinki, active across Northern European jazz and experimental music. His work spans acoustic, electroacoustic and cross-disciplinary settings.  
Teemu Mustonen — drums & percussion  
Improvising drummer and percussionist working with a modified and prepared drum set, exploring its sonic possibilities in both solo and ensemble contexts.
