MANIPULATION CHARM  
Manipulation Charm – a collaborative improvisational project between the Lithuanian experimental vocal trio Edgy Palates and the Norway-based Canadian laptop performer and programmer Mike McCormick.  
A delicate and meticulous mix of subtle melodies and unexpected vocal sounds is gently and carefully captured on stage, created here and now by Dainora Aleksaitė, Ieva Parnarauskaitė, and Jūra Elena Šedytė, and transformed by Mike McCormick’s software system written in the SuperCollider programming language. Mike, like a modern alchemist, processes the music created by Edgy Palates by passing it through a matrix of modules. What was a natural and lively voice becomes something strangely and interestingly different.

FORCES  
Constantly mutating rhythms and sharp digital textures shape the sound of Forces. Forces is the electronic music project of Finnish interdisciplinary artist Joonas Siren. Different live-coding and processing techniques are used to make dense atomized swirls - electronic and chaotic abstract oceans of sound. Their music has been released on eight international labels like Biodiversità Records, mappa, Infinite Machine and Gin & Platonic, and has also been played in DJ mixes by experimental producers like Aphex Twin and ZULI.  
For the November -26 concert Forces will prepare a special performance exploring concatenative synthesis with the programming language SuperCollider.

KOHO/KOSKENLAHTI  
Koho/Koskenlahti is a Helsinki-based duo founded by J. Koho and Minna Koskenlahti in 2024. The duo is interested in undulating at the interfaces of music and sound, exploring the sonic possibilities inherent in conventional instruments and atypical sound sources, as well as looking for different ways to unleash the sonic potential hidden in them.  
The duo has performed their context-adapted repertoire at various events and festivals. One of these performances has been released as an audio recording by Isthmus Recordings. It can be listened to behind the following link:  
[https://isthmusrec.bandcamp.com/album/kes-n-korvalla](https://isthmusrec.bandcamp.com/album/kes-n-korvalla)
