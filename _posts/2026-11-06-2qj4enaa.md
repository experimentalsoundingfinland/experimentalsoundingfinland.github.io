Himera Festival 2026  
  
At the end of fall, this year’s Himera Festival presents two days of experimental music performed by saxophonist Michaela Turcerová, composer and musician Léo Dupleix, and sho player Mattias Hållsten. The festival program will be finalized shortly.  
  
The festival will take place at the Contemporary Art Space Kutomo and Martin’s Church on November 6–7, 2026. It is organized by Himera in collaboration with Ehkä-tuotanto. The event is supported by the City of Turku.
