Expanded cinema performance — Marek Waldemar Pluciennik & Francesco Carella  
Marek Waldemar Pluciennik and Francesco Carella premiere their new piece, Constellation Fall, for 35mm film, a modified hand-cranked projector, electronics and guitar pedals. The duo has worked together for the past three years, performing across [Europe.Toinen](http://Europe.Toinen) Kerros is a volunteer-led, low threshold, DIY space for presenting, practicing, and encountering sonic art practices. Founded and currently facilitated by the association Äänen Lumo, it aims to function as a shared community-driven space. Rent and running costs of the space are partly covered by the Kone Foundation until Spring 2027.
