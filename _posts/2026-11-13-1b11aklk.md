LOVI Kirjastofest, produced by Lovi-Klubi and Rikhardinkatu Library, transforms the library building into an immersive environment where ambient music, visuals, performances, and art installations fill its spaces.  
  
Event organizers and artists from the underground scenes of Helsinki and Tampere, together with library staff, will create an unprecedented evening in which the architecture of Finland’s most beautiful library building is enriched by a synesthetic interplay of sound, image, and movement.  
  
Join us to explore, linger, and experience how today’s ambient subculture creates new meanings within a historic setting.
