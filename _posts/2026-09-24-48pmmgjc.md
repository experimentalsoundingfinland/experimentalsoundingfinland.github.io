Nikula-Kagaliagou-Kääriäinen:  
  
Clarinetist Heikki Nikula performs with chamber orchestras and as a soloist focusing on contemporary Finnish music with recitals had in several festivals, such as Helsinki & Tampere Biennali and Time of Music at Viitasaari. Heikki has played with Helsinki Philharmonics and Avanti! Chamber Orchestra since its founding in 1991. A proficient improviser, he’s also a member of Free Okapi and Nada. Releases include Hoepnadium in 1996 and Piping Down the Valleys Wild in 2003.  
  
Elena Kakaliagou is a Greek-Austrian French horn player living in Berlin. Her voice is original; lyrical and mellow, angry as a wild animal or a stormy sea, calm as the wind or the soft rain. She can be heard on more than 20 albums, with her duo Nabelóse, her trio Zinc & Copper, solo, and with plenty of collaborations. Elena is an active member of the free improv scene of Berlin. For more info: [www.elenakakaliagou.com](http://www.elenakakaliagou.com)  
  
Jukka Kääriäinen is a musician focused on experimental music, who’s working with prepared acoustic guitar or electric guitar and effects. He has performed widely across Europe and at international festivals in the UK, Spain, and France.  
  
K-L-R:  
  
K-L-R trio produces sounds in different ways that are combined into weaves finding their place in composed structures. J. Koho approaches sound, sound sources, instruments and plays to the situation. Vesa Lehko explores the boundaries of music through woodwinds, found sounds and objects. Juha Rautio plays the electric guitar and approaches music with general avant-gardism in his hand.  
  
The members of the trio have performed at various events and venues focused on experimental music, such as Asbestos Art Space, Kaekuu Festival, Käpylän lippakioski, Kalevan Erikoinen, Pertin Valinta, Rahina-klubi, Pethaus and Yö Galleria.
