The second part of the trilogy "The Public Life and Death of Afrodite “Didi” Hakkarainen" combining opera, theatre, performance and media art.   
  
The trilogy follows the life of Didi, an individual who appears emotionally volatile, from youth into middle age, portraying the experiences of a neurodivergent person navigating the complexities of contemporary society. Central themes include fragile mental health, experiences of child protection services, difficulty in interaction, and a parent’s dementia.   
  
"Didi vs. the World" is an episodic work made up of ten dramatic scenes for female voice and electric guitar. During a therapy session Didi tries to find her place in a world that shuts its ears, suppresses, and confines. Didi has met the Guitarist, in whose company it feels strangely easy to be, and who has unexpectedly become the obsessive center of her world. For the first time, Didi feels that her life has meaning.  
  
More info and tickets: [https://www.didihakkarainen.com/part-2/](https://www.didihakkarainen.com/part-2/)
