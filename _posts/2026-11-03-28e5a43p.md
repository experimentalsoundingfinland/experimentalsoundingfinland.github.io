This trio, which explores new expressive possibilities in the guitar, double bass and drums format, is made up of distinctively individual musicians. Double bassist Antti Virtaranta, who lives in Berlin, drummer Sam Ospovat, who recently moved from New York to Tuusula, and guitarist Jukka Kääriäinen, who lives in Helsinki, form a band whose repertoire consists of both composed and improvised music.  
  
Sam Ospovat – drums/percussion  
Antti Virtaranta – double bass  
Jukka Kääriäinen – electric guitar  
  
Sam Ospovat is a drummer and composer interested in the intersection of new rhythmic structures and improvisation. After delving into 20th-century percussion music and Haitian Vodou drumming, Ospovat developed song forms resembling Calder’s mobiles. The New York Times has described his work as “…prismatic, strangely seductive… influenced by 12-tone modern classical composers, the tangled energies of math rock, and the rowdy power of jazz improvisation.”  
  
Antti Virtaranta is a Finnish bassist and composer. He began his musical journey at the age of 17, focusing on jazz, and studied at the University of the Arts in Philadelphia. Soon he discovered free jazz and experimental electronic music, and it changed everything. He moved to Berlin to join the lively scene of improvisers, started projects with many musicians and developed his own sound. Currently he focuses on expressing his own voice through music and sound, by way of composition and improvisation. His musical language has been shaped by influences from jazz and rock, as well as self-taught contemporary music. Nowadays he concentrates on ideas and compositions for solo double bass and solo electronics, as well as on the use of unconventional notation. These ideas develop and move into small ensembles (duos and trios) and larger groups he is involved in, giving rise to spontaneously emerging composition in his many projects. They include collaborations with dancers and visual artists, in addition to numerous musicians.  
  
Jukka Kääriäinen is a musician focused on experimental music. Depending on the situation, Jukka’s instruments are a prepared acoustic guitar or an electric guitar and a bagful of effects. Kääriäinen has performed in many European countries and at international festivals in Britain, Spain and France. In addition to solo performances, he plays regularly in ensembles and has collaborated with many leading European improvisers, such as Teppo Hauta-Aho, Kalle Kalima, Harri Sjöström, Axel Dörner, Kriton Beyer, Rieko Okuda, Anil Eraslan, Mikko Innanen and Joel Grip. He has also given premiere performances of works by composers such as Riikka Talvitie, Sebastian Hilli, Sami Klemola, Clara de Asis and Tytti Arola.
