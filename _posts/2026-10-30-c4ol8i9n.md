The third concert of HIO’s autumn 2026 season will take place in Merihaka residents’ association’s kylätupa. The event will also serve as the launch party for the new issue of Mustapiste, a magazine dedicated to experimental art. In the words of the editorial team, "Mustapiste is an artist-led online journal and publishing platform, documenting and archiving Finland’s experimental and interdisciplinary sound art scene. #IMPROVISATION is Mustapiste's first physical publication in October 2026. Live improvisation of music has to be experienced in person, so does this special edition."  
  
The program will include free and conducted improvisation. The concerts in kylätupa will be part of Club Rahina’s program. Kylätupa is located on the deck level, opposite to the restaurant Sir Oliver, at Haapaniemenkatu 14. The concert starts at 7pm and entrance is free of charge.
